HOW TO PLAY

Paint everyone. Every paintball is a random colour. Three body hits (or two to the head) and a googly is painted out: they turn completely into the colour of the last ball, flop over, and you watch whoever painted you for 3 seconds before you come back.

Ammo is real. Your hopper holds 20 balls. Press R to reload from your reserve pods. Out of pods? Run to a yellow-ringed PAINT +40 crate on the map, then it takes 14 seconds to refill.

Hide. Bunkers, containers, logs and walls stop paintballs. C to crouch behind low cover. Paintballs drop over distance — aim a little high far away.

Win skins. The winner of the last match wears Champion Gold with a crown and a gold marker. Every match you win unlocks a new skin.
